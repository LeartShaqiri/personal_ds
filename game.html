QUMËSHTORJA VITA
2003
The Beginning
Kosovo, still healing from war, is about to witness
something remarkable. In the small town of Istog,
Devolli Group partners with Tetra Pak — bringing
UHT technology to Kosovo for the first time.

Before the factory. Before the trucks. Before the
blue cartons on every table... there were farmers.
Simple men and women who woke before dawn to
tend their cows and deliver fresh milk to their
neighbours door to door.

This is where the story of VITA begins.
