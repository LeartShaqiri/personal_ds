Our Story

Kosovo's first love.
For over twenty years.

In 2003, Devolli Group partnered with Tetra Pak to bring UHT technology to Kosovo for the very first time. The result was VITA — a milk that didn't just fill glasses, but built trust across an entire nation.

Team

280+

dedicated employees

Every drop of VITA milk passes through rigorous testing — from farm collection to packaging — ensuring unmatched purity and nutrition. We work with thousands of collaborating farmers across Kosovo's most pristine dairy regions.

"Vita is Kosovo's first milk brand produced using UHT technology. From fresh milk to yogurt, cream, and cheese, Vita embodies quality, innovation, and trust."

Today, VITA is the region's leader in dairy production and export — a position built not on shortcuts, but on an unwavering commitment to every single litre.

Our Journey

Built on belief.
Grown with trust.

2003

The Beginning

Devolli Group, in partnership with Tetra Pak, launches Kosovo's first-ever UHT milk production line. VITA is born in Istog — a small town with big ambition.

Expanding the Family

From just fresh milk, VITA grows into yogurt, ayran, clabber, cheese, cooking cream, and whipping cream — becoming the dairy that Kosovar families reach for every morning.

2010
2026

Leading the Region

Over 280 employees, thousands of partner farmers, and a presence in markets across the Balkans. Still innovating. Still uncompromising. Still VITA.

Farm to Table

Every drop
has a journey.

From the highland pastures to your table — each step is measured, tested, and cared for.

Step 01

Farm

Fresh milk collected daily from family farms across Kosovo's pristine dairy regions.

Step 02

Quality Testing

Every batch undergoes rigorous testing — purity, fat content, and microbiology.

Step 03

Production

State-of-the-art UHT processing preserves freshness and nutrition without compromise.

Step 04

Packaging

Aseptic Tetra Pak cartons lock in flavour — no preservatives, no refrigeration needed.

Step 05

Distribution

Cold-chain logistics deliver fresh to stores and homes across Kosovo and the Balkans.

Step 06

Your Table

Fresh, pure, and trusted — shared with the people who matter most.

Our Commitment

Good for your family.
Better for the future.

🌿

Tetra Pak Packaging

Our aseptic cartons are predominantly paper-based, recyclable, and designed to preserve freshness without preservatives — reducing food waste across the supply chain.

👨‍🌾

Supporting Local Farmers

Thousands of family farms across Kosovo supply our milk. We invest in training, fair pricing, and long-term partnerships that strengthen rural communities.

⚡

Efficient Production

Our UHT technology extends shelf life to months without refrigeration — dramatically reducing energy use in storage, transport, and retail compared to short-life pasteurized milk.
